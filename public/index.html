iPhone to TV

  1. Connect the iPhone and Apple TV or AirPlay TV to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Open this page in Safari, sign in, and start the movie.
  3. Tap Safari AirPlay above, or tap the AirPlay icon inside the video controls, then choose the TV.
  4. Keep Safari open on the phone while the TV plays.

iPhone works best through Safari AirPlay. Android works best through Chrome Cast when Chrome shows it.
